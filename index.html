General: Anyone experiencing discomfort such as sore eyes, cough or sore throat should consider reducing activity, particularly outdoors.
At-Risk: Adults and children with lung problems, and adults with heart problems, should reduce strenuous physical activity, particularly outdoors, and particularly if they experience symptoms. Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Dublin Air Quality Monitor is a free, open-source tool that displays real-time air quality data from publicly available Irish EPA and Dublin City Council monitoring stations. This policy explains what information, if any, is collected when you use the app.

Data We Do Not Collect

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising networks.

Geolocation

If you enable the optional location feature, your coordinates are used solely to display your position on the map within your browser. Your location is never sent to any server or stored beyond your current session.

Third-Party Data Sources

Air quality readings are fetched from the Irish Open Data API (data.gov.ie). Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on any data they may collect.
